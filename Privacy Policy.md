# Privacy Policy — Town Hall Helper

**Last updated:** September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Town Hall Helper ("the Bot") collects and how it is used. By adding or using the Bot in a Discord server, you agree to this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

The Bot may collect and store the following data as needed to provide its features:

- **Server data**: Server ID, channel IDs, and role IDs used for bot configuration.
- **User data**: User IDs, usernames, and message content only where required for a specific command or feature (e.g., logging, moderation, or reminders).
- **Command usage data**: Commands run, timestamps, and related metadata for functionality and debugging.

The Bot does **not** collect passwords, payment information, or Discord account credentials.

## 2. How We Use Information

Collected data is used only to:

- Operate and improve the Bot's features
- Maintain server-specific settings and configurations
- Troubleshoot errors and prevent abuse

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

## 3. Data Storage & Retention

Data is stored securely and retained only as long as necessary to provide the Bot's functionality. Server configuration data is deleted when the Bot is removed from a server. You may request earlier deletion (see Section 6).

## 4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share collected data with third parties, except:

- When required by law
- To Discord, as necessary for the Bot to operate via the Discord API

## 5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect stored data from unauthorized access, alteration, or loss. No method of storage is 100% secure.

## 6. Your Rights

You may request access to, or deletion of, data associated with your user ID or server by contacting us (see Section 8). Server administrators can remove the Bot at any time to stop data collection for that server.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data through the Bot, contact us to request removal.

## 8. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact: **DM Bot. Bot act as Support mail.**

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the Bot after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
